From subscription to discovery

A quieter way to keep up.

  1. 01

    Give newsletters their own address.

    Your account gets a private receiving alias. Subscribe with that address so editions arrive in UpToDate, keeping newsletter clutter out of your personal inbox.

  2. 02

    Get straight to the good part.

    Substantial stories become readable posts. Open one for more detail and original sources. Relevant source photos and figures come first; generated illustrations are a fallback.

  3. 03

    Do something with what you find.

    Ask Biggie about a post, open a recommended place in Maps, or add recipe ingredients to Reminders. Save a favorite, mark it read, and make room for the next idea.

Custom editions · connector in testing

Your curiosity.
Your own newsletter.

Use the app’s Create newsletter feature to prepare a request for Muse, with the topics and schedule you choose. Our private connector test also lets Muse deliver editions directly to your UpToDate account, without email.

Direct delivery is currently limited to approved test accounts. The public connector is being prepared for review. Schedules are confirmed and managed in Muse.
